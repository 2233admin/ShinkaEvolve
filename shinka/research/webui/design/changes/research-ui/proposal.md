# Proposal: research-ui

## Why

The project needs a requirements-driven reusable DESIGN.md before implementation.

## Problem

Read-only research console for Shinka Research: researchers and people they share it with scan a quant research direction (hypotheses, status with statistical evidence, trial count vs multiple-testing bar, budget, one-time lockbox) and its knowledge-base wiki, then drill into upstream evolution views

## Safety

Reference sites and templates are attributed evidence, not product authority or copy targets.
