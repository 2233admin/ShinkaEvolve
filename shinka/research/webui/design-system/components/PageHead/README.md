# PageHead

Kicker, title and one-sentence lede at the top of the main column.

- Kicker in `label`, `ink-muted`: what kind of page (研究方向 · `h003 · e01` · 资料库).
- Title in `display-lg` (direction, experiment) or `display-md` (workspace, library, wiki page); full-width punctuation; at most two lines.
- Lede in `body-md`, `ink-secondary`, max `measure`: one sentence telling a newcomer how to read the page, with `Term` for N, DSR, PBO, 噪声门槛 and lockbox.
- Provide: kind, title, lede. Never put figures in the lede beyond N.
